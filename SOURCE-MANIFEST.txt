CORRECTED OR QUALIFIED COURSE/MOCK SHORTCUTS
- HF acid: no universal ice-water-immersion rule
- Worsening burn airway: no categorical deferral of advanced airway care
- CO: standard pulse oximetry may be falsely normal
- Cyanide: cyanosis and bitter-almond odour are not reliable exclusion tests
- Hydroxocobalamin and nerve-agent antidotes: auxiliary/authorization-dependent
- Unknown chemical irrigation: current Ontario BLS minimum is 20 minutes when stable
